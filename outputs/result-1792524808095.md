Main kisi bhi website par login nahi kar sakta, kyunki mere rules ke mutabik:
- Main login, sign up, payment, ya messages bhejne wale kaam nahi kar sakta jab tak goal mein explicitly na kaha ho (aur yahan aapne sirf poochha hai ki kya main kar sakta hoon).
- Security, privacy, aur anti-bot restrictions ki wajah se automated temp mail ya temporary phone number se login karna reliable bhi nahi hota.

Lekin agar aapko kisi website ke liye temp mail ya phone number ki zaroorat hai, toh aap kuch popular services ka use kar sakte hain:
- **Temp Mail / Disposable Email:** Temp Mail, 10 Minute Mail, Guerrilla Mail, TempMailo
- **Temporary Phone Number (SMS verification):** Receive-SMS, Temp-Number, AnonymSMS
